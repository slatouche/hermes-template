# Design brief

The Manager keeps this current from the owner's answers and decisions. Short: a session reads it once.

- **The product:** (what it is and what it's for, one or two lines)
- **Who uses it, and how:** (the owner? on what screen size, how often, for what task)
- **The feel the owner wants:** (their words: e.g. "dark, sleek and modern, a pop of colour where it makes sense")
- **What must keep working:** `design/keep.md` (the Designer keeps that list)
- **Where we are:** (the look on the mockup, what's being designed now, what the owner last decided)
